About

SPHERIC goes Downunder

The SPHERIC World Conference is the regular assembly of experts engaged in the study and application of the smoothed particle hydrodynamics (SPH) method. In 2028 we are delighted to host the SPHERIC World Conference at Monash University in Melbourne, Australia. Monash holds a special place in the history of SPH as the long-time home of Joe Monaghan, one of the founders of SPH.

Since it's early beginnings at Monash, SPH has found widespread application beyond astronomy and astrophysics, to solid mechanics, geomechanics, coastal defence and engineering, renewable energy, manufacturing, and many others. Its meshless, particle-based framework is well suited to free-surface and multiphase flows, highly nonlinear deformation, fracture, fragmentation, and other complex physical phenomena.

We invite researchers, industry practitioners, and students from around the world to join us as we share advances in fundamental SPH research and applications.

  • Fundamental SPH formulations, consistency, and convergence
  • Multiphase, free-surface, and industrial flow applications
  • Solids, structures, and fluid–structure interaction
  • High-performance computing and emerging data-driven methods
  • Progress against the SPHERIC Grand Challenges
